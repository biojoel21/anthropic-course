# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Preparation (Vegan Athlete)

**Athlete Profile:** 178cm, 68kg, Vegan endurance athlete

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories (elevated for marathon training)
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (110g), Fat (85g), Carbs (480g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), ground flaxseed (15g), and almond milk (240ml). Side of whole wheat toast (60g) with tahini (20g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 16g, Carbs: 95g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Energy balls made with dates (80g) and almonds (30g). Orange (180g).
    * Protein: 9g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 65g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Chickpea and vegetable curry with chickpeas (150g cooked), sweet potato (150g), spinach (100g), onion (80g), and coconut milk (100ml). Brown rice (80g dry weight). Side salad with mixed greens (100g) and olive oil dressing (15ml).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 14g, Carbs: 110g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Hummus (60g) with carrot sticks (150g) and whole grain crackers (50g). Apple (160g).
    * Protein: 8g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 75g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Lentil and quinoa bowl with red lentils (100g cooked), quinoa (60g dry weight), roasted broccoli (150g), bell pepper (100g), and tahini dressing (20g). Whole wheat pita bread (70g).
    * Protein: 35g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 95g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Smoothie with plant-based protein powder (25g), banana (100g), peanut butter (20g), and oat milk (240ml).
    * Protein: 22g, Fat: 17g, Carbs: 40g
